Known disagreements to explain, not to hide:

* **Hub heights.** The report's Figure 4 text mentions hub heights above 200 m while the fleet summary gives a
  maximum of 166 m. The hub map uses the raw hub column; `df_t` applies the tip-height correction
  (`hub = raw − 0.5·rotor` above 180 m). `fleet_specs.csv` keeps `hub_raw_m` and `hub_tip_corrected` for this.
* **"R²".** 0.8641 is Pearson r² (squared correlation), not regression R² = 1 − SSE/SST; both are reported.
* **323.2 MW** is measured against the *reconstructed* clean actual (observed + estimated curtailment): a
  retrospective benchmark, not an error against observed Fingrid output.
* **Regime rule.** The report's curtailment rule "wind penetration > 45 %" is clipped in the notebook text; the
  calibration uses the notebook-2 soft gate instead (see `assumptions.md` K4).
