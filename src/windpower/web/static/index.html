How to read this

  • No calibration. No national calibration and no curtailment correction is applied to this simulation.
  • Coarse grid. The data has 0.25° resolution: a cell is about 28 km north–south by 14 km east–west. The result is the cell-average ERA5 wind converted with the chosen turbine archetype, not a micro-sited estimate for one turbine position.
  • Hub height. The 100 m wind is extrapolated to the archetype's mean hub height with a logarithmic profile and the cell's mean surface roughness, then converted with the archetype power curve (61 points, 0–30 m/s) and scaled by the installed capacity.
  • Times are UTC, hour start. Energy per hour in MWh equals the power in MW × 1 h.

          How to read this

          • No calibration. No national calibration and no curtailment correction is applied. Power in MW = capacity factor × the nominal capacity in MWp; energy in MWh = MW × 1 h; night hours are 0.
          • Coarse grid. The data has 0.25° resolution: a cell is about 28 km north–south by 14 km east–west at the selected latitude (the east–west size shrinks towards the north). The result is the cell-average irradiance, not a micro-sited estimate.
          • Fixed panel. A fixed south-facing panel (azimuth 180°) with 40° tilt, the atlite crystalline-silicon (CSi) model with inverter efficiency 0.90. Real roofs have many orientations.
          • Satellite vs. ERA5. The hybrid input uses SARAH-3 direct and diffuse irradiance only where at least 99.9 % of the cell area has valid satellite data in that hour. Northern fallback: the satellite does not cover the north and north-east of Finland (nothing north of 65° N), so those cells use ERA5 and are marked era5_fallback with the reason.
          • Times are UTC, hour start.

            Series and coverage

              Modelled national PV and Fingrid forecast

              The modelled series are uncalibrated (no national calibration, no curtailment correction); capacity comes from Fingrid dataset 267, which is not an official capacity. Fingrid 248 is a forecast, not measured generation.

              Fingrid small-scale surplus, solar (362, AV06) — different scope

              Small-scale surplus only (kWh/h shown as MW, after the producers' own consumption) — a different scope, not total generation. It has its own axis and is not comparable in size with the modelled national series; use it for the shape and timing of the daily cycle.
